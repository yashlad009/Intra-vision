# 17. Context-Based Vocabulary

Many English words have different meanings depending on the sentence.

Always understand the context before selecting the answer.

---

## Example 1

Sentence

The manager delivered a **brief** presentation.

Meaning of "brief"

A. Short

B. Important

C. Difficult

D. Loud

Answer

✅ Short

---

## Example 2

Sentence

She showed a **bright** future in research.

Meaning

A. Intelligent

B. Shining

C. Expensive

D. Dangerous

Answer

✅ Intelligent / Promising

---

## Example 3

Sentence

The project is still in its **initial** stage.

Synonym

A. Beginning

B. Ending

C. Highest

D. Final

Answer

✅ Beginning

---

## Memory Tip

Always read the complete sentence.

Never choose a word based only on its dictionary meaning.

---

# 18. Vocabulary in Reading Comprehension

Many Reading Comprehension questions ask

"What does this word mean in the passage?"

Use surrounding words as clues.

---

## Example

Sentence

Meaning

A. Ordinary

B. Extraordinary

C. Useless

D. Difficult

Answer

✅ Extraordinary

## Example

Sentence

The athlete remained **resilient** after the defeat.

Meaning

A. Weak

B. Flexible

C. Strong and able to recover

Answer

✅ Strong and able to recover

# 27. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is the difference between a synonym and an antonym?

Answer

A synonym has a similar meaning to another word, while an antonym has the opposite meaning.

---

### Q2

Why is vocabulary important in placements?

Answer

It improves reading comprehension, communication skills, writing ability, and helps solve verbal aptitude questions.

---

### Q3

How can vocabulary be improved?

Answer

Read newspapers, books, editorials, and learn new words with their meanings, synonyms, antonyms, and usage in sentences.

---

### Q4

What are root words?

Answer

Root words are the basic forms of words that help understand the meanings of many related words.

---

### Q5

Why should vocabulary be learned in context?

Answer

Because many words have multiple meanings, and context determines the correct interpretation.
