# 17. Modifier Errors

A modifier describes a noun, pronoun, or verb.

If placed incorrectly, the sentence becomes confusing or changes its meaning.

---

## Types of Modifier Errors

- Misplaced Modifier
- Dangling Modifier
- Squinting Modifier

---

## Misplaced Modifier

### Example

❌ She almost drove the car for five hours.

Meaning

She almost drove, but perhaps didn't.

Correct

✅ She drove the car for almost five hours.

---

### Example

❌ The teacher served sandwiches to the students on paper plates.

Correct

✅ The teacher served sandwiches on paper plates to the students.

---

## Dangling Modifier

### Example

❌ Walking through the park, the flowers looked beautiful.

Correct

✅ Walking through the park, I found the flowers beautiful.

---

## Rule

A modifier should always be placed close to the word it modifies.

---

# 18. Parallelism Errors

When ideas are presented in a series, all items should have the same grammatical structure.

---

## Example

❌ She likes reading, to swim, and dancing.

Correct

✅ She likes reading, swimming, and dancing.

---

## Example

❌ The project requires planning, execution, and to monitor progress.

Correct

✅ The project requires planning, execution, and monitoring progress.

---

## Rule

Maintain the same form throughout the list.

---

## Parallel Structures

Correct

```
Verb
Verb
Verb
```

Correct

```
Noun
Noun
Noun
```

Correct

```
Adjective
Adjective
Adjective
```

---

# 19. Comparison Errors

Incorrect comparisons are common in placement exams.

---

## Example

❌ Rahul is taller than any student in his class.

Correct

✅ Rahul is taller than any other student in his class.

---

## Example

❌ Gold is more valuable than silver is expensive.

Correct

✅ Gold is more valuable than silver.

---

## Example

❌ She sings better than me.

Formal English

✅ She sings better than I do.

---

## Rule

Compare similar things only.

---

# 20. Redundancy Errors

Redundancy means using unnecessary or repetitive words.

---

## Examples

❌ Advance planning

✅ Planning

---

❌ Repeat again

✅ Repeat

---

❌ Return back

✅ Return

---

❌ Final conclusion

✅ Conclusion

---

❌ Past history

✅ History

---

## Tip

Avoid repeating the same idea using different words.

---

# 21. Logical Errors

Some sentences are grammatically correct but logically incorrect.

---

## Example

❌ The child is older than his grandfather.

Grammar

Correct

Logic

Incorrect

---

## Example

❌ The train arrived before it departed.

Grammar

Correct

Logic

Incorrect

---

## Example

❌ Water boils at 20°C.

Grammar

Correct

Factually Incorrect

---

## Rule

Always check

Grammar

+

Logic

# 22. Vocabulary Errors

Wrong word choice can change the meaning of a sentence.

---

## Example

❌ She accepted his advice very politely.

Correct

✅ She accepted his advice politely.

---

## Example

❌ The medicine affected a positive effect.

Correct

✅ The medicine had a positive effect.

OR

✅ The medicine affected his health positively.

---

## Frequently Confused Words

Accept / Except

Advice / Advise

Affect / Effect

Principal / Principle

Stationary / Stationery

Compliment / Complement

Lose / Loose

Then / Than

Farther / Further

# 24. Elimination Technique

Many placement exams provide four options.

Instead of searching for the correct answer immediately,

eliminate incorrect choices.

---

## Eliminate Options That

❌ Break grammar rules.

❌ Change sentence meaning.

❌ Create tense inconsistency.

❌ Use incorrect prepositions.

❌ Break subject-verb agreement.

---

## Example

Sentence

Each employee ______ responsible.

Options

A. are

B. were

C. is

D. have

Answer

✅ C

---

## Tip

Usually,

two options can be eliminated immediately using grammar.

# 26. Shortcut Techniques

### Shortcut 1

Find the subject before checking the verb.

---

### Shortcut 2

Check tense consistency.

---

### Shortcut 3

Look for standard grammar pairs.

Examples

Neither → Nor

Either → Or

Hardly → When

No sooner → Than

Scarcely → When

---

### Shortcut 4

Check pronouns carefully.

Every pronoun should clearly refer to a noun.

---

### Shortcut 5

Read the corrected sentence naturally.

If it sounds awkward,

recheck the grammar.

---

### Shortcut 6

Don't ignore meaning.

Grammar alone isn't enough.

# 32. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is Error Detection?

Answer

Error Detection is the process of identifying grammatical, vocabulary, punctuation, or logical mistakes in a sentence.

---

### Q2

Which grammar topic is asked most frequently?

Answer

Subject-Verb Agreement is one of the most frequently tested grammar concepts.

---

### Q3

How do you solve Error Detection questions quickly?

Answer

Identify the subject, verify the verb, check tense consistency, examine pronouns, articles, prepositions, and finally verify the sentence meaning.

---

### Q4

Why is logical meaning important?

Answer

A sentence can be grammatically correct but still be logically incorrect.

Both grammar and meaning must be checked.

---

### Q5

How can grammar be improved?

Answer

Practice daily, revise grammar rules regularly, solve placement questions, and read English newspapers and books.
