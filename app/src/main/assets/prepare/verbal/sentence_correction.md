# 16. Conjunction Errors

A conjunction connects

- Words
- Phrases
- Clauses

Using the wrong conjunction changes the meaning of the sentence.

---

## Common Conjunctions

- And
- But
- Or
- Because
- Although
- Since
- While
- Yet
- Unless
- Either...Or
- Neither...Nor
- Not Only...But Also

---

## Example 1

❌ Although he was tired but he continued working.

Correct

✅ Although he was tired, he continued working.

OR

✅ He was tired, but he continued working.

Explanation

Never use

**Although + But**

together.

---

## Example 2

❌ Neither Rahul or Aman attended the meeting.

Correct

✅ Neither Rahul nor Aman attended the meeting.

---

## Example 3

❌ Either you apologize and leave.

Correct

✅ Either you apologize or leave.

---

## Placement Tip

Memorize conjunction pairs

```
Either → Or

Neither → Nor

Both → And

Not Only → But Also
```

---

# 17. Modifier Errors

A modifier describes another word.

If placed incorrectly,

the sentence becomes confusing.

---

## Misplaced Modifier

### Example

❌ She almost drove the car for six hours.

Meaning

She almost drove, but actually didn't.

Correct

✅ She drove the car for almost six hours.

---

## Dangling Modifier

### Example

❌ Walking down the road, the flowers looked beautiful.

This sentence incorrectly suggests that

the flowers were walking.

Correct

✅ Walking down the road, I saw beautiful flowers.

---

## Rule

Always place modifiers close to the word they describe.

---

# 18. Parallelism

When a sentence contains a list,

every item must have the same grammatical structure.

---

## Example 1

❌ She likes reading, to swim, and dancing.

Correct

✅ She likes reading, swimming, and dancing.

---

## Example 2

❌ The manager is responsible for hiring employees, training them, and supervision.

Correct

✅ The manager is responsible for hiring employees, training them, and supervising them.

---

## Common Parallel Structures

```
Verb

Verb

Verb
```

```
Noun

Noun

Noun
```

```
Adjective

Adjective

Adjective
```

---

# 19. Redundancy

Redundancy means

using unnecessary words.

---

## Examples

❌ Advance planning

Correct

✅ Planning

---

❌ Return back

Correct

✅ Return

---

❌ Repeat again

Correct

✅ Repeat

---

❌ Free gift

Correct

✅ Gift

---

❌ Final conclusion

Correct

✅ Conclusion

---

## Placement Tip

Read the sentence carefully.

Remove unnecessary repeated meanings.

---

# 20. Comparison Errors

Many sentence correction questions involve incorrect comparisons.

---

## Example 1

❌ Rahul is taller than any student in the class.

This includes Rahul himself.

Correct

✅ Rahul is taller than any other student in the class.

---

## Example 2

❌ Gold is more valuable than silver is expensive.

Correct

✅ Gold is more valuable than silver.

---

## Example 3

❌ She sings better than me.

Formal English

✅ She sings better than I do.

---

## Common Comparison Words

- Than
- As...As
- Like
- Unlike
- Similar To
- Different From

# 23. Elimination Technique

Placement exams usually provide

four answer choices.

Instead of searching for the correct answer immediately,

eliminate incorrect ones.

---

## Remove Options That

❌ Violate grammar rules.

❌ Change the original meaning.

❌ Introduce unnecessary words.

❌ Create tense inconsistency.

❌ Break subject-verb agreement.

---

## Example

Sentence

Neither Rahul nor his friends ____ ready.

Options

A. was

B. were

C. is

D. has

Nearest subject

friends

↓

Plural

Answer

B

## Question 1

Choose the correct sentence.

A. Neither of the boys are present.

B. Neither of the boys is present.

C. Neither of the boys were present.

D. Neither boys is present.

Answer

✅ **B**

Explanation

"Neither" is singular.

---

## Question 2

Identify the correct sentence.

A. She is senior than me.

B. She is senior to me.

C. She is more senior than me.

D. She senior than me.

Answer

✅ **B**

---

## Question 3

Choose the correct sentence.

A. The furniture are expensive.

B. The furniture is expensive.

C. The furnitures are expensive.

D. Furniture were expensive.

Answer

✅ **B**

Explanation

"Furniture" is an uncountable noun.

---

## Question 4

Choose the correct sentence.

A. Each employee have submitted the report.

B. Each employee has submitted the report.

C. Each employees has submitted the report.

D. Each employee had submit the report.

Answer

✅ **B**

---

## Question 5

Identify the correct sentence.

A. I have been living here since five years.

B. I have lived here since five years.

C. I have been living here for five years.

D. I am living here since five years.

Answer

✅ **C**

---

## Question 6

Choose the correct sentence.

A. Hardly had I entered than the bell rang.

B. Hardly I had entered when the bell rang.

C. Hardly had I entered when the bell rang.

D. Hardly entered I when the bell rang.

✅ **C**

## Question 7

C. No sooner he arrived than it started raining.

# 30. Frequently Asked Grammar Rules

---

## Rule 1

Each

Every

Everybody

Everyone

Someone

Anyone

Nobody

No one

↓

Always Singular

---

## Rule 2

Neither...Nor

Either...Or

↓

Verb agrees with the **nearest subject**.

---

## Rule 3

"As well as"

Along with

Together with

↓

Do **not** make the subject plural.

---

## Rule 4

Uncountable Nouns

Furniture

Information

Advice

Equipment

Luggage

News

Knowledge

Homework

↓

Take Singular Verbs.

---

## Rule 5

Use

"For"

with duration.

Example

For five years.

Use

"Since"

with a starting point.

Example

Since 2020.

# 32. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is Sentence Correction?

Answer

Sentence Correction is the process of identifying and correcting grammatical, structural, vocabulary, and logical errors in a sentence.

---

### Q2

Which topic is asked most frequently?

Answer

Subject-Verb Agreement and Tense Consistency.

---

### Q3

Why is tense consistency important?

Answer

Because unnecessary tense changes make sentences confusing and grammatically incorrect.

---

### Q4

What is a dangling modifier?

Answer

A modifier that incorrectly describes the wrong word or has no clear word to describe.

---

### Q5

How can Sentence Correction be solved quickly?

Answer

Identify the subject, check the verb, verify tense, examine pronouns, and finally confirm the sentence meaning.
