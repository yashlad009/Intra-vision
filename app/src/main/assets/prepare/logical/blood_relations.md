# 4. What are Blood Relations?

Blood Relations describe the relationship between members of a family.

These relationships may be

- By Birth
- By Marriage

Questions generally provide information about several people,

and you must determine how two people are related.

---

## Example

Rahul is the son of Amit.

Amit is the brother of Neha.

How is Neha related to Rahul?

Solution

Rahul

↓

Son of Amit

Neha

↓

Sister of Amit

Therefore,

Neha is Rahul's

**Aunt.**

# 8. Gender Identification

Many students make mistakes because they ignore gender.

Always identify whether the person is

- Male
- Female
- Unknown

before solving.

---

## Example

Rohan is the child of Neha.

Can we say

Rohan is Neha's son?

No.

Because the gender of Rohan is not given.

Correct Answer

Rohan is Neha's

**Child.**

## Important Rule

Never assume gender unless clearly mentioned.

# 12. Family Tree Method

The Family Tree Method is the fastest and most reliable way to solve Blood Relation questions.

Instead of remembering the entire statement,

draw a small diagram.

---

## Example

```
          Grandfather
               │
      ┌────────┴────────┐
    Amit             Neha
      │
    Rahul
```

Now it becomes obvious that

Neha is Rahul's Aunt.

---

## Why Use a Family Tree?

✔ Easy to understand

✔ Reduces confusion

✔ Prevents mistakes

✔ Useful for long questions

---

# 13. Symbol Method

Instead of writing long sentences,

use symbols.

```

F = Female

↓ Child

── Marriage
```

Example

```

 │
B (F)
 │
C (M)
```

Means

A is parent of B

B is parent of C

# 19. Coded Blood Relations

Sometimes,

instead of directly describing relationships,

symbols are used.

You must first understand what each symbol represents.

---

## Example 1

Suppose

```
A + B

means

A is the father of B

A × B

means

A is the sister of B

A − B

means

A is the wife of B
```

Question

What does

P + Q × R

mean?

### Solution

P + Q

↓

P is father of Q

Q × R

↓

Q is sister of R

Therefore,

P is also father of R.

Answer

P is the father of both Q and R.

---

## Example 2

Suppose

```
#

=

Mother

@

=

Brother

$

=

Daughter
```

Question

A # B @ C

Interpret the relations step by step.

---

## Shortcut

Never solve the complete expression together.

Break it into

small relations.

   D

Answer

E is D's

Uncle.

# 22. Reverse Solving Technique

Sometimes,

it is easier to solve from the

last person

instead of the first.

---

## Example

P is the son of Q.

Q is the daughter of R.

R is the father of S.

Find the relation between

S and P.

### Solution

Start from

P

↓

Q

↓

R

↓

S

Since

R is father of S,

S is R's child.

Q is also R's child.

Therefore,

S and Q are siblings.

Q is parent of P.

Hence,

S is

P's Uncle or Aunt

depending on gender.

Since gender is unknown,

Answer

Parent's Sibling.

---

## Memory Tip

If gender is not mentioned,

never guess.

---

# 23. Elimination Method

Instead of finding the exact answer immediately,

remove impossible options.

---

## Example

Question

How is A related to B?

Options

Father

Mother

Brother

Sister

Given

A is male.

Immediately remove

Mother

Sister

Now,

only

Father

or

Brother

remain.

Continue solving.
