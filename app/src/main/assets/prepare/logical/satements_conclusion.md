# 14. Advanced Statement & Conclusion

In advanced questions,

the statement becomes longer,

and two or more conclusions are given.

Instead of reading the conclusions first,

carefully understand the statement.

---

## Example

### Statement

All engineers in the company are graduates.

Some graduates have completed AI certification.

### Conclusions

I. Some engineers are graduates.

II. All graduates are engineers.

### Solution

Conclusion I

Follows.

Every engineer is a graduate.

---

Conclusion II

Does not follow.

The statement never says every graduate is an engineer.

---

## Answer

Only Conclusion I follows.

---

# 15. Hidden Conclusions

Sometimes,

the conclusion is not written directly.

You need to infer it logically.

---

## Example

### Statement

Every student in the class submitted the assignment before the deadline.

### Conclusions

I. No student submitted the assignment late.

II. Some students copied the assignment.

### Solution

Conclusion I

Follows.

Submitting before the deadline means no one submitted late.

Conclusion II

Does not follow.

Nothing is mentioned about copying.

---

## Memory Tip

Draw conclusions only from the information given.

Never add extra facts.

---

# 16. Fact vs Assumption

One of the biggest mistakes students make is confusing

facts

with

assumptions.

---

## Fact

Information directly stated.

---

## Assumption

Information that is believed but not stated.

---

## Example

### Statement

Rohan purchased a laptop yesterday.

### Conclusion

Rohan likes gaming.

Answer

Does not follow.

Buying a laptop does not necessarily mean he likes gaming.

---

## Another Example

### Statement

The roads are wet.

### Conclusion

It rained.

Answer

Does not follow.

Roads may be wet for many other reasons.

---

## Rule

Never assume hidden information.

---

# 17. Cause vs Conclusion

Many students confuse

Cause

with

Conclusion.

---

## Example

### Statement

Due to heavy rainfall,

schools remained closed.

### Conclusion

Heavy rainfall caused schools to close.

Answer

Follows.

---

### Wrong Conclusion

Students enjoyed the holiday.

Answer

Does not follow.

Nothing is mentioned about students' feelings.

---

# 18. Elimination Technique

Instead of proving every option,

remove clearly incorrect conclusions first.

---

## Example

### Statement

All books in the library are properly catalogued.

### Conclusions

I. Some books are not catalogued.

II. Every library book has a catalogue entry.

III. All catalogued books belong to this library.

### Solution

I

Contradicts the statement.

Remove.

III

Not supported.

Remove.

II

Directly follows.

---

## Answer

Only II follows.

### Shortcut 3

### Shortcut 4

# 24. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is the difference between a statement and a conclusion?

Answer

A statement provides information, while a conclusion is a logical result derived from that information.

---

### Q2

Why should outside knowledge be ignored?

Answer

Because the question tests logical reasoning based only on the given statement.

---

### Q3

What is an assumption?

Answer

An assumption is an unstated idea that is not directly supported by the statement.

---

### Q4

Can a conclusion be true in real life but still not follow?

Answer

Yes.

If the statement does not support it, the conclusion does not follow.

---

### Q5

What is the most important rule?

Answer

Accept the statement as true and avoid assumptions.
