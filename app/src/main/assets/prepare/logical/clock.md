# 17. Advanced Angle Problems

Some questions ask for

- Smaller Angle
- Larger Angle
- Reflex Angle

between the clock hands.

---

## Formula

```
Angle

=

|30H − 5.5M|
```

where

H = Hour

M = Minutes

---

## Example 1

Find the angle at

4:20

Solution

Hour Hand

=

30 × 4

+

0.5 × 20

=

120 +10

=

130°

Minute Hand

=

20 ×6

=

120°

Angle

=

130−120

=

10°

Answer

10°

---

## Example 2

Find the reflex angle at

4:20

Solution

Small Angle

=

10°

Reflex Angle

=

360−10

=

350°

Answer

350°

---

## Memory Tip

↓

Greater than 180°

# 27. Interview Questions

### Q1

Why does the hour hand move continuously?

Answer

Because it moves gradually every minute instead of jumping after every hour.

---

### Q2

Why is the minute hand faster?

Answer

It completes one full revolution every hour, while the hour hand completes one revolution in twelve hours.

---

### Q3

What is a reflex angle?

Answer

An angle greater than 180° but less than 360°.

---

### Q4

How does a mirror clock work?

Answer

The reflected time is obtained by subtracting the displayed time from 11:60.

---

### Q5

Which formula is used most frequently?

Answer

```
Angle

=

|30H−5.5M|
```
