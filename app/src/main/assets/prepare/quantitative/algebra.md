# 4. History of Algebra

The word

**Algebra**

comes from the Arabic word

**"Al-Jabr"**

which means

"reunion of broken parts."

The term became popular after the Persian mathematician

**Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi**

wrote the famous book

*"Al-Kitab al-Mukhtasar fi Hisab al-Jabr wal-Muqabala."*

He is often called

**The Father of Algebra.**

Later,

many mathematicians such as

- René Descartes
- Isaac Newton
- Leonhard Euler

greatly expanded Algebra into the subject we study today.

# 24. What is an Algebraic Identity?

An **Algebraic Identity** is an equation that is **always true** for every value of the variables involved.

Unlike an ordinary equation,

an identity remains true regardless of the values substituted.

---

## Example

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

This is true whether

a = 2, b = 5

or

a = 100, b = 200.

Hence,

it is called an Identity.

---

# 25. Why are Algebraic Identities Important?

Imagine multiplying

(x + 7)(x + 7)

without an identity.

You would have to perform the entire multiplication.

Using the identity,

you immediately write

x² + 14x + 49

This saves both

time

and

effort.

# 135. Age Problems using Algebra

Age problems are one of the most common aptitude topics.

The idea is simple:

Represent the unknown age by a variable and form an equation.

---

## Example 1

A father is 25 years older than his son.

If the father's age is 45 years,

find the son's age.

### Solution

Let son's age = x

Father's age = x + 25

Equation

x + 25 = 45

x = 20

Answer

Son's age = 20 years

---

## Example 2

The sum of the ages of two brothers is 34 years.

The elder brother is 6 years older.

Find their ages.

### Solution

Let younger brother = x

Elder brother = x + 6

Equation

x + (x + 6) = 34

2x + 6 = 34

2x = 28

x = 14

Elder = 20

Answer

14 years and 20 years

---
