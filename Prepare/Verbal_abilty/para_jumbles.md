# 15. Advanced Sentence Linking

Not every sentence can begin a paragraph.

Some sentences naturally depend on previous ones.

Your goal is to identify these relationships.

---

## Link Through Repeated Words

### Example

A. Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare.

B. This technology helps doctors diagnose diseases accurately.

Sentence B uses

"This technology"

which refers to

Artificial Intelligence.

Correct Pair

A → B

---

## Link Through Same Topic

A. Climate change is increasing rapidly.

B. Rising temperatures affect agriculture.

Both discuss

Climate Change.

They should remain together.

---

## Rule

Whenever a sentence begins with

- This
- These
- Such
- They
- It

look for the sentence that introduces that noun.

---

# 16. Chronological Arrangement

Some paragraphs follow

Time Order.

Arrange events from

Earlier

↓

Later.

---

## Example

A. He joined the company.

B. He received training.

C. He became a manager.

Correct Order

B → A → C

Training

↓

Joining

↓

Promotion

---

## Another Example

A. Seeds were planted.

B. Plants grew.

C. Fruits were harvested.

Correct Order

A → B → C

---

## Time Clues

Look for words like

- First
- Then
- Next
- Afterwards
- Finally
- Later
- Eventually

---

# 19. Transition Word Analysis

Transition words connect ideas smoothly.

Understanding them makes Para Jumbles much easier.

---

## Addition

- Moreover
- Furthermore
- Also
- In addition
- Besides

These continue the previous idea.

---

## Contrast

- However
- Although
- Yet
- Nevertheless
- On the other hand

These introduce opposite ideas.

---

## Result

- Therefore
- Hence
- Thus
- Consequently
- As a result

These indicate conclusions.

---

## Example

A. The company invested heavily in employee training.

B. Consequently, productivity increased significantly.

Correct Order

A → B

---

## Example

A. The proposal looked attractive.

B. However, its implementation was difficult.

Correct Order

A → B

# 20. Paragraph Completion

Instead of arranging sentences,

you choose the sentence that best completes the paragraph.

---

## Example

Artificial Intelligence is becoming an essential part of modern industries. It improves efficiency and reduces repetitive work. However, organizations must also consider ethical concerns.

Which sentence best completes the paragraph?

A. Responsible AI development requires transparency and human oversight.

B. AI is only useful for gaming.

C. Every job will disappear immediately.

D. Machines should replace all people.

Answer

✅ A

---

## Strategy

Choose the sentence that naturally extends the author's idea.

# 22. Elimination Technique

When four options look confusing,

remove impossible arrangements.

---

## Eliminate

❌ Paragraphs starting with pronouns.

❌ Conclusions placed at the beginning.

❌ Cause after effect.

❌ Example before explanation.

---

## Example

Sentence

"Therefore, the project succeeded."

Cannot be the opening sentence.

---

## Example

Sentence

"For example, electric vehicles reduce pollution."

Cannot start a paragraph because

an example needs a previous statement.

# 24. Shortcut Techniques

### Shortcut 1

Find the independent sentence first.

It usually becomes the opening sentence.

---

### Shortcut 2

Look for pronoun references.

Pronouns always refer back to previously introduced nouns.

---

### Shortcut 3

Identify transition words.

They reveal the logical relationship.

---

### Shortcut 4

Arrange

General

↓

Specific

↓

Example

↓

Conclusion

---

### Shortcut 5

Keep concluding words

like

Therefore,

Hence,

Finally,

at the end.

---

### Shortcut 6

Read the completed paragraph once.

It should sound natural and coherent.

---

# 25. Common Mistakes

❌ Starting with a sentence containing pronouns.

---

❌ Ignoring transition words.

---

❌ Placing examples before explanations.

---

❌ Reversing cause and effect.

---

❌ Choosing grammatically correct but logically disconnected sequences.

---

❌ Reading only individual sentences instead of understanding the paragraph as a whole.

# 29. Paragraph Completion Strategy

Sometimes,

only the final sentence is missing.

---

## Example

Artificial Intelligence is transforming education by providing personalized learning experiences. Students can learn at their own pace using adaptive software. Teachers also receive valuable insights into student performance.

Choose the best concluding sentence.

A. Therefore, AI has the potential to improve both teaching and learning.

B. AI is only useful in gaming.

C. Schools should stop using teachers.

D. Technology has no role in education.

Answer

✅ A

---

## Rule

A concluding sentence should

- Summarize the discussion
- Maintain logical flow
- Avoid introducing a completely new idea

# 31. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is a Para Jumble?

Answer

A Para Jumble is a verbal ability question where sentences are arranged in a logical order to form a meaningful paragraph.

---

### Q2

How do you identify the opening sentence?

Answer

The opening sentence introduces the topic and usually does not contain pronouns or transition words referring to previous ideas.

---

### Q3

Why are transition words important?

Answer

They indicate relationships such as contrast, addition, result, or sequence, helping determine the correct order.

---

### Q4

What is the biggest mistake students make?

Answer

They focus only on grammar instead of the logical flow and relationships between ideas.

---

### Q5

What is the fastest strategy?

Answer

Identify the opening sentence, connect related sentence pairs, place examples after explanations, and keep conclusions at the end.
