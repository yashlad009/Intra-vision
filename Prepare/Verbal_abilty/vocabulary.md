# 16. Context-Based Vocabulary

Many English words have multiple meanings.

The correct meaning depends on the sentence.

Always understand the context first.

---

## Example 1

Sentence

The manager gave a **brief** presentation.

Meaning

Short

Not

Important

---

## Example 2

Sentence

She has a **bright** future.

Meaning

Promising

Not

Shining

---

## Example 3

Sentence

The scientist made a **remarkable** discovery.

Meaning

Extraordinary

---

## Rule

Read

Entire Sentence

↓

Understand Context

↓

Choose Meaning

---

## Memory Tip

Context is more reliable than memorization.

# 32. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is vocabulary?

Answer

Vocabulary is the collection of words a person understands and uses while speaking, reading, listening, and writing.

---

### Q2

What is Active Vocabulary?

Answer

Words you confidently use in daily communication.

---

### Q3

What is Passive Vocabulary?

Answer

Words you understand but rarely use.

---

### Q4

How can vocabulary be improved?

Answer

Read daily, maintain a vocabulary notebook, revise regularly, and practice using new words in conversations and writing.

---

### Q5

Why are root words important?

Answer

Root words help you understand the meanings of many related words without memorizing each one individually.

---

### Q6

Why should words be learned in context?

Answer

Because the same word can have different meanings depending on how it is used.
