# 36. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is a Number Series?

Answer

A sequence of numbers arranged according to one or more logical mathematical rules.

---

### Q2

What is the first thing you should check?

Answer

Difference between consecutive numbers.

---

### Q3

When should you use the ratio method?

Answer

When the differences are inconsistent or the numbers increase rapidly.

---

### Q4

Why are prime numbers important?

Answer

Many placement questions use prime-number patterns.

---

### Q5

How do you solve hidden pattern questions?

Answer

Check differences, ratios, squares, cubes, prime numbers, Fibonacci, alternate patterns, and combined operations systematically.
