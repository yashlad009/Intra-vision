# 15. Advanced Ranking Problems

Advanced ranking questions involve multiple people and several conditions.

Instead of solving each condition separately,

combine all the given information systematically.

---

## Example

Eight students are standing in a line.

Rahul is 3rd from the left.

Aman is 2nd to the right of Rahul.

Neha is immediately left of Aman.

Question

Who is standing between Rahul and Aman?

### Solution

Rahul

↓

3rd Position

Aman

↓

5th Position

Neha

↓

4th Position

Answer

Neha

---

## Tip

Draw a straight line and mark positions.

Avoid solving mentally.

---

# 16. Missing Rank Questions

Sometimes,

the rank of one person is missing,

and you must calculate it using the given information.

---

## Example

A person is

18th from the left

and

23rd from the right.

Find the total number of persons.

### Formula

```
Total Persons

=

Left Rank

+

Right Rank

−1
```

### Solution

18

+

23

−1

=

40

Answer

40 Persons

---

## Memory Trick

Subtract

1

because the same person is counted twice.

---

# 17. Relative Position Problems

These questions ask

Who is ahead?

Who is behind?

How many persons are between two people?

---

## Example

A is 7th from the left.

B is 12th from the left.

How many persons stand between them?

### Formula

```
Difference

−1
```

Solution

12−7−1

=

4

Answer

4 Persons

---

## Example

Ravi is 9th from the right.

Karan is 5 positions ahead of Ravi.

Find Karan's position from the right.

Solution

9−5

=

4th

Answer

4th from the right

---

# 19. Circular Ranking

Circular ranking arranges people around a circle.

Unlike linear ranking,

there is no fixed first or last position.

---

## Example

Eight friends are sitting around a circular table.

Rahul sits immediately clockwise to Amit.

Priya sits opposite Rahul.

Question

Who sits opposite Rahul?

Answer

Priya

---

## Tip

Fix one person at any position.

Arrange the remaining people relative to that person.

---

# 20. Elimination Technique

Instead of calculating every position,

remove impossible options.

---

## Example

Question

A is ahead of B.

B is ahead of C.

Who can be first?

Options

A

B

C

D

Since

A is ahead of both

B

and

C,

Answer

A

No detailed calculation required.

# 23. Shortcut Techniques

### Shortcut 1

Always identify

the reference point.

Left,

Right,

Top,

or

Bottom.

---

### Shortcut 2

Use formulas instead of counting manually.

---

### Shortcut 3

Draw a line for linear arrangements.

Draw a circle for circular arrangements.

---

### Shortcut 4

Subtract

1

when finding the total number of persons.

---

### Shortcut 5

For people between two positions,

Use

Difference−1

### Shortcut 6

Solve fixed positions first,

then fill remaining positions.

# 26. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is the difference between rank and position?

Answer

A rank indicates a person's place in an ordered list, while a position refers to the specific location relative to others.

---

### Q2

Why is

−1

used in the total persons formula?

Answer

Because the same person is counted once from the left and once from the right.

---

### Q3

What is the biggest mistake in ranking questions?

Answer

Counting the same person twice or confusing left and right positions.

---

### Q4

Why should diagrams be drawn?

Answer

They simplify visualization and reduce logical errors.

---

### Q5

How can ranking questions be solved faster?

Answer

Use formulas, identify the reference point, and draw simple diagrams.
