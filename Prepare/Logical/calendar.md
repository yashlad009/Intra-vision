# 16. Repeating Calendars

A calendar repeats after a fixed interval.

However,

not every year has an identical calendar because leap years affect the day sequence.

---

## Ordinary Year

An ordinary year has

365 days.

365 ÷ 7

=

52 weeks + 1 day

Therefore,

every ordinary year contributes

**1 Odd Day.**

---

## Leap Year

A leap year has

366 days.

366 ÷ 7

=

52 weeks + 2 days

Therefore,

every leap year contributes

**2 Odd Days.**

---

## Important Observation

Two years have identical calendars only if

✔ Both are ordinary years

OR

✔ Both are leap years

and

✔ The total odd days between them is a multiple of 7.

---

# 18. Calendar Matching

Two years are said to have matching calendars when

- Every date falls on the same weekday.
- The arrangement of months is identical.

---

## Example

Find the next year after 2023 having the same calendar.

### Solution

2023 is an ordinary year.

Count odd days until another ordinary year matches.

This concept is frequently asked in placement exams.

---

## Shortcut

Remember

Leap years interrupt the calendar cycle.

Always check leap years first.

# 27. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is an Odd Day?

Answer

The remaining day after counting complete weeks.

---

### Q2

Why do leap years exist?

Answer

To keep the calendar synchronized with the Earth's revolution around the Sun.

---

### Q3

Why is modulo 7 used?

Answer

Because a week contains exactly seven days.

---

### Q4

What is the biggest mistake in Calendar questions?

Answer

Ignoring leap years and odd days.

---

### Q5

How can Calendar questions be solved faster?

Answer

Use odd days, ignore complete weeks, and check leap years first.
