# 18. Advanced Floor Puzzles

In advanced floor puzzles,

multiple people live on different floors with several conditions.

---

## Example

A building has six floors.

A, B, C, D, E and F live on different floors.

Conditions

- A lives above C.
- D lives on the top floor.
- F lives immediately below D.
- B lives below C.

Question

Who lives on the second floor?

### Solving Strategy

Step 1

Place definite clues.

Top Floor

↓

D

Immediately below

↓

F

Step 2

Use relative clues.

Continue until every floor is filled.

---

## Tip

Always number floors before solving.

Example

```
6

5

4

3

2

1
```

---

# 19. Advanced Box Puzzles

Box puzzles involve arranging objects in a particular order.

Questions may ask

- Top Box
- Bottom Box
- Middle Box
- Immediate Above
- Immediate Below

---

## Example

Five boxes

A, B, C, D and E

are stacked vertically.

Conditions

- A is above C.
- D is below B.
- E is the topmost box.

Question

Which box is at the bottom?

---

## Strategy

Draw the stack first.

Never solve mentally.

# 24. Grid Method

The Grid Method is one of the fastest puzzle-solving techniques.

---

## Example

| Person | Delhi | Mumbai | Pune |
|---------|-------|---------|------|
|A| | | |
|B| | | |
|C| | | |

Mark

✔

for confirmed.

Mark

✖

for impossible.

This immediately reduces possibilities.

---

## Advantages

✔ Easy visualization

✔ Fewer mistakes

✔ Faster elimination

---

# 25. Reverse Solving Technique

Sometimes,

the easiest way is to work backwards.

---

## Example

Question

Who sits immediately to the left of Rahul?

Instead of finding Rahul first,

start from the confirmed seat and move backwards using the clues.

---

## Memory Tip

If forward solving becomes confusing,

reverse the process.

# 31. Interview Questions

### Q1

What is the best approach for solving Logical Puzzles?

Answer

Organize the information using a table or grid and solve systematically.

---

### Q2

Why should definite clues be solved first?

Answer

They reduce possibilities and make the remaining clues easier to place.

---

### Q3

Why is elimination useful?

Answer

It removes impossible options, reducing complexity.

---

### Q4

Should you guess in puzzle questions?

Answer

No. Always rely on confirmed information.

---

### Q5

Which method is most effective for multi-variable puzzles?

Answer

The Grid Method.
